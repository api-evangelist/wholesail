# Wholesail

> Wholesail is an all-in-one accounts receivable and B2B payments platform for
> food & beverage wholesalers, distributors, and service vendors. It helps
> vendors manage credit risk, automate collections, run customer onboarding,
> accept ACH and card payments, and drive autopay — connected to the vendor's
> ERP/accounting system. Wholesail is a fintech company backed by Foundry Group.
> As of this writing Wholesail publishes no public developer API; it integrates
> with ERP systems using their native API protocols, file exchange, and RPA.
